# OUR IMPACT
## Two Decades of Inspiring Healthier Hearts

Since its establishment on 1 September 2004, Heart Care Foundation Comilla has been working to prevent cardiovascular diseases and stroke through public awareness, community engagement, free healthcare initiatives, education and international collaboration.

Inspired by the global World Heart Day movement, the Foundation has transformed its commitment into sustained community action, reaching people across Cumilla and beyond.

### Our Journey Through the Years

**2000 – The Beginning of the World Heart Day Movement**

The global World Heart Day initiative began in 2000 under the leadership of the World Heart Federation, with support from the World Health Organization. The inaugural theme was *“I Love My Heart, Let It Beat.”* This global initiative inspired the growing movement for cardiovascular health awareness.

**2001–2002 – Building Awareness for a Heart-Healthy Life**

With the theme *“A Heart for Life,”* attention was drawn to the importance of cardiovascular health at every stage of life, encouraging healthy habits from childhood.

**2003 – Raising Awareness of Women's Heart Health**

The theme *“Women, Heart Disease & Stroke”* highlighted the importance of recognizing cardiovascular disease and stroke as major health concerns for women.

**2004 – Establishment of Heart Care Foundation Comilla**

On 1 September 2004, Heart Care Foundation Comilla was established under the leadership of Professor Dr. Triptish Chandra Ghose in Cumilla, Bangladesh. The Foundation began its mission of promoting healthier hearts and longer, more productive lives. The global World Heart Day theme was *“Children, Adolescents and Heart Disease.”*

**2005–2007 – Promoting Healthy Lifestyles**

- **2005:** *Healthy Weight, Healthy Shape* – Promoting healthy body weight and preventing obesity.
- **2006:** *How Young Is Your Heart?* – Encouraging physical activity and tobacco avoidance.
- **2007:** *Team Up for Healthy Hearts* – Inspiring families and communities to work together for heart health.

**2008–2010 – Understanding and Managing Heart Disease Risks**

- **2008:** *Know Your Risk* – Highlighting cardiovascular risk factors such as hypertension, cholesterol and diabetes.
- **2009:** *Work with Heart* – Promoting heart-healthy workplaces.
- **2010:** *I Work with Heart* – Continuing workplace awareness and celebrating the 10th anniversary of World Heart Day.

**2011–2015 – Making Heart Health a Community Priority**

- **2011–2012:** *One World, One Home, One Heart* – Encouraging heart-healthy choices at home and across communities.
- **2013:** *Take the Road to a Healthy Heart.*
- **2014:** *Heart Choices NOT Hard Choices.*
- **2015:** *Healthy Heart Choices for Everyone, Everywhere.*

**2016–2019 – Empowering People to Become Heart Health Champions**

- **2016:** *Power Your Life.*
- **2017:** *Share the Power.*
- **2018:** *My Heart, Your Heart.*
- **2019:** *Be A Heart Hero.*

These campaigns encouraged individuals and families to take responsibility for their cardiovascular health.

**2020–2022 – Keeping Hearts Connected During the COVID-19 Pandemic**

Even during the global COVID-19 pandemic, the importance of cardiovascular health remained central to the global awareness movement.

- **2020:** *Use Heart to Beat Cardiovascular Disease.*
- **2021:** *Use Heart to Connect.*
- **2022:** *Use Heart for Every Heart.*

**2023 – International Recognition and a Historic Achievement**

The theme was *“Use Heart, Know Heart.”*

Heart Care Foundation Comilla received the prestigious **Most Inspiring World Heart Day Award 2023** from the World Heart Federation, recognizing its outstanding World Heart Day campaign.

Another significant milestone was the inclusion of the Bengali language in World Heart Federation's World Heart Day communication and campaign materials following the Foundation's initiative and request. This achievement represented an important moment for Bengali-speaking communities worldwide.

**2024 – Turning Awareness into Action**

Under the theme *“Use Heart for Action,”* the global campaign emphasized the need for meaningful action, stronger health policies and collective efforts to prevent cardiovascular diseases.

### Our Impact at a Glance

**100,000+ People Reached Through Free Healthcare Initiatives**

Since 2004, the Foundation has organized more than a hundred free heart camps, providing free medical consultations and healthcare services to over 100,000 people from underprivileged and underserved communities.

**Community-Based Awareness Campaigns**

Our awareness initiatives extend beyond seminars and conferences. Through public rallies, cycling rallies, school and college activities, physical exercise programmes, educational leaflet distribution and billboards, we promote cardiovascular health across Cumilla and beyond.

**14 Published Books on Health Awareness**

Professor Dr. Triptish Chandra Ghose has authored 14 books on health awareness and related subjects, including seven books focused on heart health and cardiovascular disease prevention.

**Full Member of the World Heart Federation**

Heart Care Foundation Comilla is a full member of the World Heart Federation, connecting our local community-based initiatives with the global movement for cardiovascular health.

### Our Commitment Continues

Our journey is not just about the years behind us; it is about the lives we can positively influence in the years ahead.

We remain committed to building a healthier society through cardiovascular disease prevention, awareness, education, rehabilitation and community engagement.

**Heart Health for Everyone. A Healthier Future for All.**
